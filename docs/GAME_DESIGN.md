# MORI Cat Rescue 2D — Game Design

## High Concept
A cozy rescue shelter management game where cats arrive with unknown needs and personalities, recover under the player's care, then leave for carefully matched permanent homes.

## Core Loop
rescue case -> intake -> care -> observe -> discover personality -> prepare adoption profile -> receive applicant -> interview -> evaluate compatibility -> approve/reject/conditions -> adoption -> follow-up -> adoption wall

## Player Actions
- click/tap to move
- inspect cat
- refill food/water
- clean litter
- give enrichment
- review intake cases
- manage room capacity
- interview adopters
- approve/reject adoption
- assign foster homes
- buy supplies/upgrades

## Cat Needs
0–100:
- hunger
- thirst
- energy
- stress
- hygiene
- trust
- play
- health

## Personality
Examples:
shy, confident, playful, lazy, curious, clingy, independent, territorial, gentle, vocal, foodie, mischievous, fearful, social.

Traits begin hidden or uncertain and are revealed through observations.

## Rescue Cases
- stray adult
- kitten
- senior
- bonded pair
- mother + kittens
- owner surrender
- fearful cat
- injured cat
- returned adoption
- transfer from another shelter

## Adoption Gameplay
Applicants have:
- household
- children
- pets
- housing
- secure windows/balcony
- schedule
- budget
- experience
- expectations
- travel habits

Interview questions reveal responsibility, patience, knowledge, flexibility, red flags, and contradictions.

## Adoption Result
- strong match
- acceptable with conditions
- investigate more
- poor match
- reject

## Post-Adoption
Follow-up at:
- 3 days
- 1 week
- 1 month

Outcomes:
- thriving
- adapting
- concern
- returned

Returned cats are recoverable, not game-over.

## Shelter Progression
Suggested rooms:
1. reception/intake
2. main cat room
3. quarantine
4. clinic
5. nursery
6. socialization room
7. adoption room
8. storage
9. staff room
10. outdoor enclosure

Every room must unlock a mechanic.

## Adoption Wall
Successful adoptions add:
- portrait
- cat name
- rescue date
- adoption date
- family label
- latest follow-up
